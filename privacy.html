Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-01

This privacy policy describes how the satirical-shorts application ("the App"), operated by Florent Guillaud ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you connect your TikTok account to upload generated videos.

The App is a personal-use tool that generates short vertical videos from news articles and uploads them to the user's own TikTok account drafts.

1. Information we collect

When you authorize the App to access your TikTok account, we receive and store:

  • OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token) issued by TikTok
  • Basic profile information that TikTok exposes via the user.info.profile scope (display name, profile link, etc.)
  • Video files that you have generated locally and elected to upload

We do not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Passwords (TikTok's OAuth flow never shares your password with us)
  • TikTok content from accounts other than yours
  • Browsing history, contacts, or device identifiers

2. How we use the information

  • OAuth tokens are used solely to authenticate API calls to TikTok on your behalf, in particular to upload videos to the inbox of your account via the Content Posting API.
  • Profile information is used to confirm the identity of the authenticated account and is not shared.
  • Video files are sent to TikTok's upload endpoints exclusively; copies remain on your local machine and are not transmitted elsewhere by us.

3. Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the user's machine in a .env file. They are not transmitted to any server controlled by us.
  • Generated videos and intermediate artifacts (script, audio, alignment) live in the user's local out/ directory.
  • The refresh token is valid for approximately 365 days; afterwards, you must re-authorize.

4. Third-party services

To produce a video, the App calls third-party APIs. Each provider has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini (script generation, TTS, image relevance scoring) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Pexels (b-roll video search) — pexels.com/privacy-policy
  • TikTok Content Posting API (final upload) — tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

Article URLs that the user submits are fetched, parsed, and any embedded images / videos may be downloaded for use as visual material. We do not redistribute them outside of the user's own TikTok upload.

5. Your rights

Because the App stores TikTok tokens locally on your machine, you can revoke access at any time by:

  • Deleting the .env file or removing the TIKTOK_REFRESH_TOKEN line
  • Revoking the App's access in your TikTok account settings (tiktok.com/setting/manage-app-permissions)

You can request deletion of any data we may hold by contacting us at the address below.

6. Children

The App is not intended for users under 13. Do not authorize the App if you are under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
