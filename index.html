satirical-shorts

A personal-use Node.js app that generates short vertical videos from news articles and uploads them as drafts to the operator's TikTok account.

Maintained by Florent Guillaud — florent.guillaud@gmail.com

What it does

  1. Scrapes a news article URL (text + images + embedded videos)
  2. Generates a short, satirical script with Google Gemini
  3. Synthesizes voice-over with Gemini TTS
  4. Aligns the audio with whisperX for word-level captions
  5. Fetches relevant b-roll videos from Pexels
  6. Composes a 1080×1920 / 30 fps MP4 with Remotion (kinetic captions, Ken Burns, multi-clip cuts)
  7. Uploads the final video to the operator's own TikTok drafts via the official Content Posting API

Scope

  • Personal, non-commercial use only. A single operator (the developer) uploads to their own TikTok account.
  • Not a service offered to third parties.
